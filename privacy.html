Privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026. This explains what Crema stores about you, why, and how to have it removed.

What Crema stores

  • Your account: your email address and the username you choose. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with Crema. Crema never sees your Google password.
  • Your journal: the trades, notes, playbook, accounts, settings and chart screenshots you add, so you can use them on any device you sign in on.

Who can see it

  • Only you. Your journal is private to your account and isn’t shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Crema doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

Services Crema uses

  • Supabase stores your account and journal.
  • Vercel hosts the app.
  • Google handles “Continue with Google” sign-in.
  • Fonts and a few libraries load from Google Fonts and public code libraries (cdnjs, jsDelivr).

On your device

Crema also keeps a copy of your journal and settings in your browser so it works quickly and offline. Clearing your browser data removes that copy; the copy in your account stays.

Removing your data

You can delete trades and journal entries inside Crema at any time. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email sinlex46@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. It’s removed within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: sinlex46@gmail.com.
